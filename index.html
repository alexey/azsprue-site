What it does

Your bench, sorted.

The paint rack, the kit box and the shopping list, all in one place. Works offline, right at the workbench.

Your whole paint rack

8,000+ paints from 20 brands are built in: Vallejo, Tamiya, AK, Mr Hobby, Army Painter, Citadel, Humbrol and more. Anything missing? Add your own.

Scan, don't type

Point the camera at your bottles, one after another. Unknown barcode? The app reads the label, you pick the paint once, and it's known from then on.

Every kit knows what it needs

Add a model, keep the instructions with it and list its paints. See at a glance what you have, what's missing and what you can swap.

Snap the paint chart

Photograph the paint table from the instructions and AI reads it for you, with your own OpenAI key. You check every line before anything is saved.

Out of it? Use what's close

The kit calls for a paint you don't have? See equivalents from other brands, with the ones already on your shelf first.

One list for the shop

Everything your kits are missing plus your wishlist, in one list. Mark paints as bought, drop what you don't need, or share it.

Backups you control. Save everything to iCloud Drive or Files, or pick a folder for automatic backups. Restore after a preview, with undo. Export and import your paints as CSV.

Your stash stays yours.

No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything lives on your iPhone and in the backups you make. The only things that leave it are the ones you send: pages to OpenAI with your own key, and unknown barcodes if you turn on the online lookup.
